Trip Packing List Notes

For carry-on-only trips, liquids over 100ml are not allowed through security, including that giant bottle of sunscreen. Buy sunscreen after landing instead.

Always pack one full change of clothes and any daily medication in the carry-on, never in checked luggage. Checked bags occasionally get delayed by a day, and you don't want to be without either of those.

A portable charger over a certain watt-hour rating (usually around 100Wh) may need to go in carry-on rather than checked luggage, and very large ones may not be allowed on the plane at all. Check the specific airline's rules before flying.
